About the data: the storm on screen is a procedural simulation (this page can't reach NOAA's servers), but the two rendering modes model the real sources faithfully. Wide views sample a 1 km Cartesian grid at a 2-minute cadence — MRMS composite reflectivity, pulled as tiles from NOAA's map services. Past the zoom threshold the panel switches to NEXRAD Level 2 super-resolution from the KDMX (Des Moines) radar: polar gates 0.5° × 250 m fetched from the open AWS noaa-nexrad-level2 bucket — note the radial beam texture and the 230 km range ring. On the real device the ESP32-S3 would fetch pre-rendered tiles over Wi-Fi and blit them to the 800×480 panel.
