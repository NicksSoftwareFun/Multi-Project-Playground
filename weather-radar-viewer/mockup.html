About the data: this hosted page can't reach NOAA (the artifact sandbox blocks all external requests), so everything on screen is a faithful simulation of the real feeds. Left of NOW: MRMS composite reflectivity sampled on a true 1 km grid, switching to KDMX NEXRAD Level 2 polar gates (0.5° × 250 m) when zoomed in. Right of NOW: HRRR simulated reflectivity (REFD) on the model's 3 km grid at 15-minute steps — on hardware these come as ready-made XYZ tiles from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet. The SAT button switches to a separate full-screen GOES-East GeoColor view — shown as a raw image, not overlaid on the map, since the real GOES frame is in geostationary projection and doesn't line up with a flat map. On hardware that image comes live from NOAA STAR (cdn.star.nesdis.noaa.gov). For actual live data in a browser, open live.html from this repo locally — no sandbox, real tiles.
