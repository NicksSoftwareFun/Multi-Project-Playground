HOW TO FLY

  • STEER Drag anywhere. The ship follows your thumb, not the spot you touch.
  • GRAZE Skim close to hazards without touching them. Grazing fills the heat bar and raises your multiplier.
  • OVERDRIVE Cells and grazes charge it. Tap the round button — or a second finger anywhere — to smash through everything for five seconds at triple score.
  • SHIELDS Each hit costs one. Blue pickups give one back.
  • SECTORS Every 1500 m the corridor changes hands. It gets faster, tighter and stranger.

Keyboard: arrows or WASD to steer, space for overdrive, P to pause.
